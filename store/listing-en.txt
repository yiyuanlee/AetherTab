----- Detailed description (paste below this line) -----

AetherTab replaces Chrome's new tab page with a glassmorphic workspace for saving and organizing tabs.

Drag an open tab from the sidebar into a collection. AetherTab saves the page and closes that tab so the window stays clear. Open anything back with one click.

What you can do
• Drag open tabs into collections, then close them automatically
• Group saved pages by site into collapsible folders
• Filter inside a single collection without searching the whole workspace
• Check local weather from the header, with GPS or a city search
• Switch between dark and light themes
• Keep collections on this browser with Chrome storage. No account.

Who it is for
People who keep dozens of tabs open for work, research, or side projects and want them sorted without signing in.

Privacy
AetherTab uses the tabs permission to list, save, and close tabs. Collections, theme, and weather settings stay in chrome.storage on your device. Geolocation is optional and only used for the weather card; you can type a city instead. Weather forecasts come from Open-Meteo. Optional GPS reverse-geocoding uses BigDataCloud. Favicons use Chrome's favicon API. AetherTab does not create accounts and does not upload your collections to our servers.
